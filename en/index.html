How we work

Two ways of working, one standard of design

The difference between our on-site and online service is not the quality of the design: it is who takes the measurements and who follows the site. Everything else — analysis, layout, materials, lighting, budget — is identical.

01 — In person

On-site design in Verona and province

We come to you. A full measured and photographic survey, a check on the constraints, real ceiling heights and out-of-square walls recorded. From there we build the design and, if needed, coordinate joiners, fitters and suppliers through to handover.

  • Professional survey carried out by us, returned as a dimensioned plan: that is the €100 site visit, deducted from the quote if the work is commissioned
  • Showroom visits and physical sample selection together
  • Purchasing management and trade coordination
  • Coverage: Verona city, Lake Garda, Valpolicella, eastern Verona, the lowlands and Lessinia

How online consultation works
The heart of what we do

Online consultation isn't a reduced version. It's a different method.

Plenty of e-design services sell a mood board generated in half an hour. We work the other way round: verified survey first, then layout, then style. The result is a design that stands up on site, not only on Pinterest.

The full process Book the first call
01Day 0

Orientation call — 45 minutes

We look at the property, the habits of the people who live there, the constraints and the real budget. You leave with a direction, not a generic quote.

02Days 1–4

Guided survey and photo dossier

We send forms and a guide for measuring walls, heights, openings, sockets and drains correctly. We verify everything together before drawing anything.

03Days 5–12

Layout and concept

Two or three dimensioned layout options with stated pros and cons. Once a route is chosen, palette, materials and finishes follow, with codes and references so you can request samples from the retailer.

04Days 13–20

Design, renders and shopping list

Dimensioned drawings, lighting and socket plan, materials schedule, shopping list with models, quantities, prices and links. Everything you need to order without surprises.

05Afterwards

Purchasing support

We stay available to review the quotes you receive, handle variations and site surprises. On request we manage orders and deliveries ourselves.

    Frequently asked

    The questions everyone asks

    How much does an online interior design consultation cost?

    The first introductory call, short and with no obligation, is free: it lets us understand the project and update the quote. After that: €90 for a 45-minute orientation call with a written report, from €190 for a floor-plan consultation on a room up to 15 m², from €490 for a complete furnishing design. Prices rise in floor-area bands and are identical on a video call and in person.

    Does it really work without you seeing the property?

    Yes, if the survey is done properly — which is why we don't leave it to chance. We send forms, a guide on how to measure (including out-of-square walls, which almost nobody records) and a precise list of photographs. Then we check everything together on a call before drawing anything.

    There is an honest limit: if the work touches structure, building services or planning permissions, a technician on site is still required. In that case we work alongside your own surveyor or architect, or handle the visit ourselves if you are in the Verona area.

    Do I have to buy furniture through you?

    No, and the choice stays yours. The shopping list gives brand, model, finish, quantity and list price: you can buy wherever you like, including from a retailer near you. If you would rather we managed orders, lead times and deliveries, that is a separate service, quoted openly.

    How long does it take?

    For a single room: 2–3 weeks from the orientation call. For a complete apartment: 4–7 weeks. Timings depend mostly on how quickly the survey and your feedback arrive — the part that is on your side. Two rounds of revisions are included per stage; further rounds are quoted before being carried out.

    Do you work with international clients and second homes?

    Yes. We work with owners of second homes around Lake Garda and in Valpolicella, with calls arranged across time zones. Consultations and deliverables are in Italian.
