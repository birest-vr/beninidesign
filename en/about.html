About us

Benini Design

Interior design and furnishing, based in Verona. We work on real spaces, with real budgets, for people who want to feel genuinely at home.

A home doesn't have to impress the people who walk in. It has to work for the people who live in it, every day, and go on working for years.

It is a simple principle, but it concretely changes the way we work: we start from the survey, not the mood board; we decide where the sockets are needed before choosing a wall colour; and we let go even of a beautiful piece when, in that space, it has no place or no function. The aesthetics come afterwards. And precisely for that reason, when they arrive, they hold up better.

Our work takes two forms. In Verona and its province we work directly on site: we survey the spaces, meet the people and follow the project closely. Across the rest of Northern Italy we work remotely, by video call, keeping the same method and the same deliverables and organising only the survey stage differently.

A good interior design shows after two years, not after two weeks.How we judge our own work
Principles

Four rules we don't bend

01

Measure first

No drawing before a verified survey. It's tedious, it delays the start by a few days, and it saves entire projects.

02

Budget is a design constraint

Not a detail to face at the end. If the budget doesn't support the design, we change the design — we don't pretend prices are different.

03

Always alternatives

Every material we propose comes with a cheaper and a dearer option, with the difference explained. The choice stays yours.

04

Usable documents

A drawing a joiner has to interpret over the phone is a badly made drawing. Dimensioned, legible, self-contained.

How we work together

Projects go best when

  • There is a reference budget, however modest: knowing the figure lets us steer every choice well
  • We can talk openly about daily habits, which are the real raw material of a design
  • There is room to settle the layout before the aesthetics
  • Timescales are reasonable: a considered design is measured in weeks
Let's discuss it first

Worth talking through

  • If you only need a visualisation, with no intention of building it: we can take that on, but it is a different service from an interior design project
  • If your timescale is very tight: we'll tell you straight away what is realistically possible to do and deliver
  • If you would rather delegate every choice: let's define together beforehand how much room to leave for discussion and how much to entrust to us
  • If the absolute priority is spending as little as possible: in that case it is right to compare several quotes and consider different solutions
  • If you need planning applications, structural calculations or site supervision: we work with qualified professionals we can hand that work to
Let's talk

The simplest way to find out whether we can work well together.

A first video call, short and free, to hear what you need, bring the priorities into focus and see whether the way we work suits you. At the end, you receive an updated quote and the possible next steps.
